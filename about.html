About Us

Welcome to AplaRecharge, a dedicated digital utility bill payment and mobile recharge platform founded and operated by Moreshwar Kiran Patil, based in Pandharpur, District Solapur, Maharashtra (PIN - 413304).

Our mission is to make everyday digital utility payments—including Prepaid Mobile Recharges, DTH Television Recharges, Electricity Bill Payments (MSEDCL & other distribution companies), Postpaid Mobile Bills, Broadband Fiber recharges, and NHAI FASTag top-ups—simple, transparent, and instant for citizens across Maharashtra and India.

Why Choose AplaRecharge?

  • Instant Processing: 99.9% of recharges are completed within 5 seconds with live operator reference IDs.
  • Transparent INR Pricing: Customers pay the exact plan MRP in Indian Rupees (₹) with zero hidden fees.
  • Automated Refund Protection: Any failed or dropped recharge is automatically reversed to the user's wallet or original payment method.
  • Dedicated Local Support: Direct customer assistance via phone and email from our Pandharpur office.
