Refund & Cancellation Policy

Effective Date: November 2026 | Last Updated: November 2026

At AplaRecharge (operated by Moreshwar Kiran Patil, Pandharpur, Dist - Solapur, Maharashtra - 413304), customer satisfaction and payment safety are our top priorities. Below is our transparent policy governing cancellations and refunds for all transactions made on our website and mobile application.

1. Cancellation Policy

  • Because mobile recharges, DTH top-ups, and utility bill payments are processed in real-time with telecom and utility operators, a transaction that has already been successfully processed and confirmed by the operator cannot be cancelled or reversed.
  • Users are advised to verify the mobile number, subscriber ID, and plan amount before confirming payment.

2. Failed Recharge / Bill Payment Refund Policy

  • Instant Wallet Refund: If a recharge or bill payment fails immediately due to an operator error or server downtime, 100% of the deducted amount is automatically refunded to the customer's AplaRecharge Wallet instantly.
  • Pending Transactions: If a transaction remains in a "Pending" state due to telecom operator timeout, it is automatically resolved within 2 to 24 hours. If marked failed by the operator, the full amount is refunded automatically.
  • Original Source / Bank Account Refund: Where a payment is debited from a customer's bank account / UPI via our Payment Gateway (such as PhonePe PG) for a failed order, or if the customer requests a refund to their original payment source, the refund is initiated within 24 hours and credited back to the customer's original bank account / UPI source within 3 to 5 working days as per banking guidelines.
